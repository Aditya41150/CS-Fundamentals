## Study Rule
Do not memorize scheduler tables or deadlock definitions first. Understand the problem each mechanism is trying to solve and then learn the terminology.

## Interview-Ready Test
You should be able to explain process vs thread, context switching, virtual memory, deadlock, mutex vs semaphore, and one scheduling algorithm with a simple example.
