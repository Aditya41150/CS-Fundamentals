# Interview Prep Hub

Use this section after learning the fundamentals. It is for **retrieval practice, project discussion, mock interviews and HR preparation**.

## Weekly Interview Routine

### Technical
Pick 5–10 questions from the subject you studied that week. Answer without notes. Mark weak answers for revision.

### DSA
Do at least one timed problem during the week and explain your approach aloud as if an interviewer is listening.

### SQL
Write queries from scratch. Do not rely on autocomplete or solutions until you have tried yourself.

### Projects
For every project on your resume, prepare to explain:
- the problem;
- why you chose the stack;
- your exact contribution;
- important technical decisions;
- bugs/challenges;
- what you would improve.

### HR / Behavioral
Use [HR Interview Questions](../HR-Interview-Questions.pdf) for question ideas. Prepare concise, truthful answers for introduction, strengths/weaknesses, teamwork, failure, conflict, motivation and career goals.

## Before an Interview

1. Review your resume.
2. Review your projects.
3. Revise the CS topics most likely to be asked.
4. Solve 1–2 moderate DSA problems.
5. Write a few SQL queries.
6. Do a 15–30 minute mock interview.

## During a Mock Interview

Answer first. Do not immediately check notes. Afterward, record what you could not explain, where you got stuck, and which answer needs revision.

## Final Rule

The goal is not to memorize a question bank. Question banks are for discovering gaps in your understanding.
